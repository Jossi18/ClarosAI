          FAQ

          Frequently asked questions.

          How long until the first agent goes live?

          Allow four to six weeks from the start of the audit to the first agent going live. The next ones are faster, because the mapping is already done.

          Do we need to change our tools?

          No. The agents plug into Gmail, Outlook, your CRM, your website or your shared documents. Your team keeps working in the same tools.

          What if the agent gets it wrong?

          Anything that commits your business (a quote, a reply to a negative review, a sensitive message) needs approval before it’s sent. You choose what the agent can do on its own.

          Where is our data stored?

          Your documents and conversations stay in your own workspace, hosted in Europe. They are never used to train public models.

          How much does it cost?

          The audit is a fixed fee. Each agent then has a setup cost and a monthly subscription covering hosting, model usage and maintenance. You get the quote at the end of the audit.
